A grocery list that does the sorting

Handla is a grocery shopping list app for iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo. Type a few letters, pick a suggestion, and the item lands in the right section of the store with its own illustration. Check things off as you shop and the list gets shorter as you walk.

When you know what you’re cooking, add the recipe instead. Its ingredients go onto your list, and the recipe waits there as your meal plan, with timers built into its steps for when you start cooking.

Is Handla free?

Handla is free to download and use for a list of up to ten items. Handla Premium, an in-app subscription with a 7-day free trial, removes the limit and adds as many lists as you like and unlimited saved recipes. The price is always shown in the app before you commit, and you can cancel anytime.

How does Handla sort my list?

Every item you add goes into its store section, such as Produce, Dairy or Bakery, so you walk the store once instead of zigzagging. You can drag the sections into the order of your own store.

Can I share or print a list?

Yes. Send a list as text to anyone, in Messages, Mail or any other app, or print it. Recipes and whole meal plans can be shared as a link, and printed with or without photos to save on ink. Sharing sends a copy, so it doesn’t keep a list in sync between people.

Can I add a recipe to my grocery list?

Yes. Add a recipe to a list and its ingredients go straight onto it, sorted into store sections and scaled to the number of people you cook for. The recipe stays on the list as your meal plan, ready when it’s time to cook.

Can I save recipes from websites, Instagram and TikTok?

Yes. Paste a link to a recipe page, an Instagram Reel or a TikTok, or share it to Handla from Safari or the app, and Handla saves the recipe with its ingredients and steps.

Can I cook without touching the screen?

Yes. Full-screen cooking mode can read each step aloud, listens for commands like “next step”, and turns the page when you raise your hand. Timers are built into the steps, so starting one is a single tap.

Does Handla have widgets?

Yes. Put a list or your meal plan on the Home Screen, and keep the next meal or a list’s count on the Lock Screen.

Does Handla work on iPad and iPhone Duo?

Yes. Handla is made for iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo. Unfold the Duo and your lists fill the big screen, with an optional sidebar and a card layout made for the extra space.

Which languages does Handla speak?

English, Swedish, Danish, German, Spanish, Finnish, French, Icelandic, Japanese, Norwegian and Dutch, grocery names included. Läs mer på svenska →

Is Handla available on Android?

No. Handla is an app for iPhone and iPad.

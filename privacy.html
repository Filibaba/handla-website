Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

What Handla stores, what it sends, and what it never touches.

The short version

Your lists, recipes and settings are stored on your device. There’s no Handla account, so there’s nothing for us to log you in to.

Handla collects no usage statistics, shows no ads and uses no advertising identifiers. Something leaves your device only when you ask for it: saving a recipe from a link, sharing a list, a recipe or a meal plan, or subscribing to Premium. Each is described below.

What Handla stores, and where

Everything you create in Handla lives on your device: your lists and their items, the recipes you save or write, your meal plans and your settings. It isn’t uploaded to us, and we can’t see it.

Apple Watch

Handla on Apple Watch shows your lists and meal plan. Your iPhone sends them straight to your paired Apple Watch over Apple’s own connection to the watch, not through any server of ours.

When you add items by voice on the watch, watchOS’s own dictation turns your words into text. Dictation is Apple’s service and falls under Apple’s privacy policy; Handla only gets the text.

Saving recipes from links

When you save a recipe from a website, Handla fetches that page itself and reads the recipe out of it on your device. The site sees a request, as it would from any browser.

Saving an Instagram Reel or a TikTok goes through our own server at media.plantry.app, which fetches the post and passes back its video and caption. We send that server the link you shared and nothing about you. The finished recipe is stored on your device.

Sharing lists, recipes and meal plans

When you share a list, Handla hands its text to the app you pick, such as Messages or Mail; nothing goes through us. When you share a recipe or a meal plan as a link, Handla uploads that recipe or plan, with its photos, to our server at recipe.handlagrocerylist.app so the link has something to show. Anyone with the link can open it. Nothing else from your device is sent, and the upload doesn’t carry your name or Apple Account. Write to us if you want a shared link taken down.

Camera, microphone and speech

Handla asks for these only when you first use Add by Voice or turn on hands-free cooking. You can refuse and still use everything else.

Add by Voice. Turns what you say into items on your list, using the microphone and speech recognition. Turn it on in Settings and tapping + starts listening; there’s also a microphone beside the add field. Recognition runs on your device, with iOS’s own speech model. Until that model has been downloaded for your language, iOS’s older speech recognizer stands in, and Apple may then process the audio on its servers, under Apple’s privacy policy. Handla itself never stores or uploads the audio.

Camera. Watches for a raised hand so you can turn the page while cooking. Gesture detection runs entirely on your device, and the frames are never stored or uploaded.

Voice commands while cooking. Listens for short commands such as “next step” while you cook hands-free. Recognition runs on your device. The audio isn’t stored and isn’t uploaded.

Subscriptions and payments

Handla Premium is sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card details, your billing address or your Apple Account. To check whether a subscription is active, Handla uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase receipt and a random identifier that isn’t linked to your name or email. Refunds are Apple’s to issue, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Email

If you write to us, we keep your message and your address so we can reply and follow up if the same problem comes back. We don’t add you to a mailing list and we don’t pass your address on.

Who else is involved

The complete list: Apple, for the App Store, payments, speech recognition and dictation; RevenueCat, for checking subscriptions; and the websites and social platforms you choose to save a recipe from. There are no advertising networks, no data brokers and no tracking pixels. We don’t sell, rent or trade personal information, and we never have.

Children

Handla isn’t aimed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct or erase it, and complain to your data protection authority. The answer is usually short, because almost everything Handla knows about you never leaves your device. Email hello@filibaba.com and we’ll answer.

The data controller is Filibaba AB, Sweden.

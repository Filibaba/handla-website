Privacy Policy

What Handla stores, what it sends, and what it never touches.

The short version

Your lists, recipes and settings are stored on your device, and in your own iCloud account when you sync or share. There’s no Handla account, so there’s nothing for us to log you in to.

Handla collects no usage statistics, shows no ads and uses no advertising identifiers. Something leaves your device only when you ask for it: saving a recipe from a link, sharing a recipe or a meal plan as a link, or subscribing to Premium. Each is described below.

What Handla stores, and where

Everything you create in Handla lives on your device: your lists and their items, the recipes you save or write, your meal plans and your settings. It isn’t uploaded to us, and we can’t see it.

With Premium, your lists sync between your devices through Apple’s iCloud, in your own iCloud account. When you share a list, it’s shared through iCloud with the people you invite. That data belongs to your Apple Account and passes through Apple, not through us.

Saving recipes from links

When you save a recipe from a website, Handla fetches that page itself and reads the recipe out of it on your device. The site sees a request, as it would from any browser.

Saving an Instagram Reel or a TikTok goes through our own server at media.plantry.app, which fetches the post and passes back its video and caption. We send that server the link you shared and nothing about you. The finished recipe is stored on your device.

Sharing recipes and meal plans

When you share a recipe or a meal plan as a link, Handla uploads that recipe or plan, with its photos, to our server at recipe.handlagrocerylist.app so the link has something to show. Anyone with the link can open it. Nothing else from your device is sent, and the upload doesn’t carry your name or Apple Account. Write to us if you want a shared link taken down.

Camera, microphone and speech

Handla asks for these only when you turn on hands-free cooking, and you can refuse and still use everything else.

Camera. Watches for a raised hand so you can turn the page while cooking. Gesture detection runs entirely on your device, and the frames are never stored or uploaded.

Microphone and speech recognition. Listens for short commands such as “next step”. Recognition runs on your device. The audio isn’t stored and isn’t uploaded.

Subscriptions and payments

Handla Premium is sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card details, your billing address or your Apple Account. To check whether a subscription is active, Handla uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase receipt and a random identifier that isn’t linked to your name or email. Refunds are Apple’s to issue, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Email

If you write to us, we keep your message and your address so we can reply and follow up if the same problem comes back. We don’t add you to a mailing list and we don’t pass your address on.

Who else is involved

The complete list: Apple, for the App Store, payments and iCloud; RevenueCat, for checking subscriptions; and the websites and social platforms you choose to save a recipe from. There are no advertising networks, no data brokers and no tracking pixels. We don’t sell, rent or trade personal information, and we never have.

Children

Handla isn’t aimed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct or erase it, and complain to your data protection authority. The answer is usually short, because almost everything Handla knows about you never leaves your device. Email hello@filibaba.com and we’ll answer.

The data controller is Filibaba AB, Sweden.

Changes to this policy

When it changes we’ll update this page and the date below. The terms of service that cover Handla alongside our other apps are at filibaba.com.
